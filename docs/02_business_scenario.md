# 02 — Business Scenario

## Status
**Working hypothesis for Day 1.** This is intentionally provisional and will be challenged during market/ICP research before implementation.

## Fictional product
An **AI-powered inbound lead qualification and routing platform for B2B SaaS companies**.

### Product concept
The platform connects to a company's CRM, website forms, calendars, and internal messaging tools. It helps revenue teams:
- enrich inbound leads/accounts;
- apply qualification rules;
- route leads to the correct owner;
- trigger time-sensitive follow-up;
- surface routing/data-quality exceptions;
- maintain an auditable decision trail.

## Why this scenario is useful for the portfolio
It creates a realistic GTM-engineering problem with observable account attributes and signals while remaining understandable to an employer.

The scenario supports testing of:
- firmographic fit
- sales-team maturity
- CRM/technology signals
- hiring/growth signals
- operational-complexity proxies
- RevOps/Sales Ops personas
- confidence-aware scoring

## Initial ICP hypothesis — NOT YET VALIDATED
Potential target accounts:
- B2B SaaS/software companies
- approximately 50–500 employees
- English-speaking commercial motion
- meaningful inbound and/or outbound sales operation
- modern CRM in place
- dedicated sales/revenue operations responsibilities, or evidence of growing GTM complexity

Potential target personas:
- VP / Head of Revenue Operations
- Head of Sales Operations
- VP Sales / CRO in smaller companies
- Revenue Systems / GTM Systems owner

Potential observable signals to research:
- hiring SDRs/AEs/RevOps roles
- sales-team headcount growth
- recent funding or expansion
- CRM technology usage
- RevOps/Sales Ops hiring
- website conversion infrastructure
- new market/region expansion
- leadership changes

## Important rule
None of the above becomes a scoring factor merely because it sounds plausible. Each factor must pass a later review:
1. Is it logically connected to the product problem?
2. Is it observable with acceptable coverage?
3. Is the source reliable enough?
4. Can the field be normalized?
5. Does it add information beyond another field?
6. Is its cost justified?
7. Does evaluation suggest it improves prioritization?

## Working GTM question
> Given a broad list of B2B software companies, which accounts appear sufficiently aligned with the hypothetical product's problem and current buying context to justify expensive contact-level enrichment and human outbound attention?

## Ethical/portfolio disclosure
The project demonstrates targeting-system design. It will not present inferred sensitive personal traits, fabricate intent, or claim that public signals prove purchase intent. Signals are treated as prioritization hypotheses, not facts about a company's willingness to buy.
